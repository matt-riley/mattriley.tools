# Under Pressure

The catalogue is an independent print exhibition on warm paper. Its three inks are charcoal, vermilion and ultramarine. Use Anton for condensed display lettering, a serif for captions and body copy, and JetBrains Mono for specimen data.

The opening spread pairs a large title with a tool portrait. The exhibition includes registration marks, sheet turns, an engraver’s loupe and downloadable proofs. Respect reduced-motion preferences. The complete searchable index follows the selected plates and stays accessible from the masthead.

New tools, plugins and templates come from the generated catalogue. A custom plate is optional. Omit northeast-pointing arrows from the site interface.
